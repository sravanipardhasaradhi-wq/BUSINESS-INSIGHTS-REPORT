# TASK 4: BUSINESS INSIGHTS REPORT (EDA)

## PROJECT OVERVIEW

This project focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a Marketing Campaign dataset using Python. The objective was to analyze campaign performance, understand customer conversion behavior, and identify which marketing channels generate the highest Return on Investment (ROI).

Instead of building a Machine Learning model, this project emphasizes understanding the business problem and providing actionable recommendations based on data insights.

The analysis helps answer the business question:

**"Which marketing channels should receive more budget based on ROI?"**

---

## OBJECTIVE

* Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on marketing campaign data.
* Clean and validate the dataset.
* Analyze campaign performance across different marketing channels.
* Visualize the marketing funnel from Impressions to Conversions.
* Identify channels generating the highest ROI.
* Provide data-driven recommendations for budget allocation.

## KEY FINDINGS

### 1. Search Channel Generated the Highest ROI

Search campaigns achieved the highest average ROI among all marketing channels, making them the most cost-effective option.

### 2. Email Campaigns Delivered Strong Performance

Email campaigns consistently generated high ROI while maintaining relatively low campaign costs.

### 3. Display Campaigns Generated the Highest Revenue

Display advertising contributed the highest overall revenue among all channels.

### 4. Social Media Campaigns Produced the Lowest ROI

Social campaigns showed the lowest ROI and may require optimization before increasing investment.

### 5. Conversion Rates Were Similar Across Channels

Conversion rates remained relatively stable across different marketing channels, indicating comparable customer conversion efficiency.

---

## BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

* Increase budget allocation toward Search campaigns.
* Continue investing in Email marketing campaigns.
* Optimize Social Media campaigns before increasing spending.
* Maintain Display campaigns for revenue generation.
* Regularly monitor funnel metrics to improve conversion performance.
